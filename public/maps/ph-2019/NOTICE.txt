Philippine administrative boundary snapshot (historical 2019 directory)

Source: https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps/tree/8eeead560246863c8c820c31ca6fbca81a279477/2019
Medium-resolution source GeoJSON; retained administrative properties, coordinates rounded to six decimal places.

Underlying boundaries: credit NAMRIA and PSA, through OCHA's Philippines COD-AB, CC BY-IGO 3.0.
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-phl
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

This compatibility snapshot preserves the application's historical nine-digit PSGC joins.
It does not represent current administrative divisions. Some features contain earlier source validity dates.
Do not substitute a new vintage without validating code correspondence and feature-property changes.
